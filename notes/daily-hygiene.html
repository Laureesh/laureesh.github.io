☀️ Morning — get ready

Optional finish: a little fragrance, following the product label. Keep it away from your face, private areas, and irritated skin.

Shower guide — work from top to bottom

  1. Hair — wash days only. Shampoo the scalp, rinse, condition, and gently detangle. Start around once a week for tightly coiled hair; adjust to your hair and scalp.
  2. Face. With clean hands, gently massage in CeraVe cleanser and rinse. Avoid rough scrubbing.
  3. Upper body. Behind ears → neck → shoulders → chest → belly and belly button → back → arms → armpits.
  4. Lower body. Legs → knees → ankles → feet and between toes.
  5. Private areas. With clean hands, gently wash the groin and outer skin. Use water on the head of the penis; use mild, fragrance-free cleanser on outer skin only if tolerated. If uncircumcised, gently retract the foreskin, rinse underneath, and replace it.
  6. Buttocks & external anal area — last. Use clean hands or a separate washcloth. Wash gently and rinse; do not put soap inside. Wash your hands afterward.

After rinsing: use a clean towel and a separate clean face towel. Dry underarms, groin folds, buttock crease, and between toes thoroughly. Avoid thick lotion in moist folds.

🌙 Night — wind down

As needed: shower after sweating or getting dirty; gently clean your tongue. For nighttime antiperspirant, follow the label and apply to completely dry underarms.

Keep it simple

  • Floss once a day. Morning or night—choose the time you’ll remember.
  • No automatic second shower. An evening shower is for when you need it.
  • Be gentle. Avoid aggressive scrubbing, scraping, or pulling your hair.
  • Outdoors? Reapply sunscreen every 2 hours and after sweating or swimming, following the label.
  • Mouthwash is optional. Use fluoride rinse at a separate time from brushing.
