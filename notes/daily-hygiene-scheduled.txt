MY DAILY HYGIENE ROUTINE
Simple daily care • Morning, night, and weekly

Schedule: Eastern Time. Daily tasks reset at midnight; weekly tasks reset on Sunday.

☀️ MORNING — EVERY DAY

□ Teeth & tongue
Brush for 2 minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
Spit out toothpaste; avoid rinsing with water. Gently clean your tongue.

□ Mouthwash — optional
Use Crest Pro-Health Advanced Extra Whitening according to your bottle.
Its published label says 10 mL for 1 minute, twice daily after brushing.
Spit out; do not swallow. Avoid food and drink for 30 minutes afterward.

□ Shower & face
Use warm water, Dove Sensitive Skin body wash, and CeraVe Foaming Facial Cleanser.
Follow the shower guide below. Dandruff wash days: Sunday and Wednesday.

□ Dry & moisturize
Pat dry. Carefully dry underarms, groin folds, and between toes.
For your face, gently pat with a fresh Clean Skin Club Clean Towel XL; discard after use.
Apply body lotion to slightly damp skin; avoid thick lotion in moist folds.

□ Underarms
Apply deodorant or antiperspirant to completely dry skin.
Follow the product label.

□ Sunscreen & lips
Apply CeraVe AM Facial Moisturizing Lotion SPF 30 to face, ears, and neck.
Use SPF 30 lip balm.

□ Hair & clean clothes
Gently style your hair.
Put on fresh underwear, socks, and a clean shirt.

Optional: a little fragrance. Avoid your face, groin, and irritated skin.


🌙 NIGHT — EVERY DAY

□ Clean your face
When needed to remove sunscreen or makeup, start with DHC Deep Cleansing Oil.
With dry hands, massage 2–3 pumps onto a dry face. Add water until milky, then rinse.
Follow with CeraVe Foaming Facial Cleanser and rinse gently.
CeraVe alone is fine when one cleanse is enough. Skip repeat cleansing if already done in the shower.
Pat dry with a fresh Clean Skin Club Clean Towel XL; discard after use.

□ Moisturize face & lips
Apply CeraVe PM Facial Moisturizing Lotion to your face and neck.
Apply a thin layer of Vaseline to your lips.

□ Floss & brush
Floss if you haven’t already today.
Brush for 2 minutes with fluoride toothpaste. Spit; avoid rinsing with water.

□ Mouthwash — optional
Use Crest Pro-Health Advanced Extra Whitening following your bottle's directions.
For the published Advanced Extra Whitening label: 10 mL, swish for 1 minute, then spit.
Do not swallow. Avoid food and drink for 30 minutes afterward.

□ Prepare for tomorrow
Wear clean sleepwear.
Set out fresh clothes and hang your towel to dry.

As needed:
• Shower after sweating, exercising, or getting dirty.
• Gently clean your tongue.
• Apply nighttime antiperspirant if directed by its label.


🧺 WEEKLY — FRESH START

My reset day: Sunday. A new weekly checklist starts each Sunday.
Dates update automatically in Notebook.

□ Sunday — Wash bedsheets and pillowcases.
□ Sunday — Dandruff shampoo & gentle detangling.
Use Dove Men+Care Dandruff Defense 2-in-1 on your scalp, following the bottle.
Rinse well. Add a separate conditioner to hair lengths if needed; detangle gently.
□ Monday — Clean and trim fingernails.
□ Tuesday — Clean combs, brushes, and styling tools.
□ Wednesday — Dandruff shampoo.
Second weekly scalp wash with Dove Men+Care Dandruff Defense 2-in-1.
Follow the bottle; condition hair lengths if needed.
□ Wednesday — Wash hats, durags, and bonnets as needed.
□ Thursday — Maintain facial hair with clean tools.
□ Friday — Clean shoes as needed and let them dry between wears.
□ Saturday — Check supplies and replace anything running low.
Check Clean Towels, mouthwash, AM/PM moisturizers, cleansers, and shampoo.
Check your remaining Whitestrips treatments against the course instructions.

Between resets:
• Tuesday and Friday: check whether your towels need washing.
• Wash towels after about 3 uses—sooner if damp, dirty, or smelly.
• Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months, or sooner if worn.


✨ WHITENING — OPTIONAL, LIMITED COURSE

Crest 3D Whitestrips Professional Effects — your 22-treatment / 44-strip kit.
Suggested time: early evening, before nighttime brushing, only during your course.
Follow the exact wear time, frequency, and course length on your box and sachets.
Some kits contain different treatment types; do not assume all 22 have the same wear time.
Do not brush immediately before applying strips. Never extend wear time to speed results.
If sensitivity or gum irritation develops, pause; stop and contact your dentist if it persists.

🚿 SHOWER GUIDE — HEAD TO TOE

1. HAIR — SUNDAY & WEDNESDAY WHILE TREATING DANDRUFF
Use Dove Men+Care Dandruff Defense 2-in-1 on the scalp.
Massage and rinse according to the bottle, including any stated contact time.
The dandruff label recommends at least twice weekly unless your clinician directs otherwise.
Condition hair lengths separately if needed and gently detangle tightly coiled hair.
If your scalp worsens or does not improve with directed use, seek medical advice.

2. FACE
Use clean hands and CeraVe Foaming Facial Cleanser.
Massage gently for 30–60 seconds, then rinse. Avoid scrubbing.

3. UPPER BODY
Behind ears → neck → shoulders → chest → belly and belly button
→ back → arms → armpits.

4. LOWER BODY
Legs → knees → ankles → feet → between toes.

5. PRIVATE AREAS
With clean hands, gently wash the groin, external penis, and scrotum.
Use water on the head of the penis.
Use mild, fragrance-free cleanser on outer skin only if tolerated.
If uncircumcised, gently retract the foreskin, rinse underneath,
and replace it.

6. BUTTOCKS & EXTERNAL ANAL AREA — LAST
Use clean hands or a separate washcloth.
Wash gently and rinse well. Keep soap outside the anus.
Wash your hands afterward.

FINISH
Pat your body dry with a clean bath towel.
Pat your face with a fresh Clean Skin Club Clean Towel XL, then discard it.
A clean, soft reusable face towel also works.
Dry skin folds and between toes thoroughly.

REMEMBER

• Floss once a day—morning or night.
• A second shower is only needed when you’re sweaty or dirty.
• Be gentle: avoid aggressive scrubbing, scraping, or pulling hair.
• Outdoors, reapply sunscreen every 2 hours and after sweating
  or swimming, following the label.
• Mouthwash is optional. This Crest rinse has product-specific directions;
  follow your bottle rather than the previous general rinse-timing advice.
• Whitestrips are a limited course. The daily/weekly calendar does not track
  your treatment count or automatically choose a whitening end date.

These weekdays are a convenient plan, not strict deadlines. Do tasks sooner when needed.
Keep it consistent. Optional steps can stay optional.
