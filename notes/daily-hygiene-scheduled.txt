MY DAILY HYGIENE ROUTINE
Simple daily care • Morning, night, and weekly

Schedule: Eastern Time. Daily tasks reset at midnight; weekly tasks reset on Sunday.

☀️ MORNING — EVERY DAY

□ Teeth & tongue
Brush for 2 minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
Spit without rinsing immediately. Gently clean your tongue.

□ Shower & face
Use warm water, Dove body wash, and CeraVe face cleanser.
Follow the shower guide below. Wash hair only on wash days.

□ Dry & moisturize
Pat dry. Carefully dry underarms, groin folds, and between toes.
Apply body lotion to slightly damp skin; avoid thick lotion in moist folds.

□ Underarms
Apply deodorant or antiperspirant to completely dry skin.
Follow the product label.

□ Sunscreen & lips
Apply your SPF 30 facial moisturizer to face, ears, and neck.
Use SPF 30 lip balm.

□ Hair & clean clothes
Gently style your hair.
Put on fresh underwear, socks, and a clean shirt.

Optional: a little fragrance. Avoid your face, groin, and irritated skin.


🌙 NIGHT — EVERY DAY

□ Clean your face
Wash away sunscreen, oil, and dirt.
Skip this if you just washed your face during a shower.

□ Moisturize face & lips
Apply CeraVe PM to your face and neck.
Apply a thin layer of Vaseline to your lips.

□ Floss & brush
Floss if you haven’t already today.
Brush for 2 minutes. Spit without rinsing immediately.

□ Prepare for tomorrow
Wear clean sleepwear.
Set out fresh clothes and hang your towel to dry.

As needed:
• Shower after sweating, exercising, or getting dirty.
• Gently clean your tongue.
• Apply nighttime antiperspirant if directed by its label.


🧺 WEEKLY — FRESH START

My reset day: Sunday. A new weekly checklist starts each Sunday.
Dates update automatically in Notebook.

□ Sunday — Wash bedsheets and pillowcases.
□ Sunday — Wash and condition hair when due; gently detangle.
□ Monday — Clean and trim fingernails.
□ Tuesday — Clean combs, brushes, and styling tools.
□ Wednesday — Wash hats, durags, and bonnets as needed.
□ Thursday — Maintain facial hair with clean tools.
□ Friday — Clean shoes as needed and let them dry between wears.
□ Saturday — Check supplies and replace anything running low.

Between resets:
• Tuesday and Friday: check whether your towels need washing.
• Wash towels after about 3 uses—sooner if damp, dirty, or smelly.
• Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months, or sooner if worn.


🚿 SHOWER GUIDE — HEAD TO TOE

1. HAIR — SUNDAY, OR YOUR NEXT WASH DAY
Shampoo your scalp, rinse, condition, and gently detangle.
For tightly coiled hair, start around once weekly and adjust to your needs.

2. FACE
Use clean hands and CeraVe cleanser.
Massage gently for 30–60 seconds, then rinse. Avoid scrubbing.

3. UPPER BODY
Behind ears → neck → shoulders → chest → belly and belly button
→ back → arms → armpits.

4. LOWER BODY
Legs → knees → ankles → feet → between toes.

5. PRIVATE AREAS
With clean hands, gently wash the groin, external penis, and scrotum.
Use water on the head of the penis.
Use mild, fragrance-free cleanser on outer skin only if tolerated.
If uncircumcised, gently retract the foreskin, rinse underneath,
and replace it.

6. BUTTOCKS & EXTERNAL ANAL AREA — LAST
Use clean hands or a separate washcloth.
Wash gently and rinse well. Keep soap outside the anus.
Wash your hands afterward.

FINISH
Pat dry with a clean towel. Use a separate clean face towel.
Dry skin folds and between toes thoroughly.

REMEMBER

• Floss once a day—morning or night.
• A second shower is only needed when you’re sweaty or dirty.
• Be gentle: avoid aggressive scrubbing, scraping, or pulling hair.
• Outdoors, reapply sunscreen every 2 hours and after sweating
  or swimming, following the label.
• Mouthwash is optional. Use fluoride rinse at a separate time
  from brushing.

These weekdays are a convenient plan, not strict deadlines. Do tasks sooner when needed.
Keep it consistent. Optional steps can stay optional.
