# My daily hygiene routine

**Morning → Night → Weekly reset**
Keep the daily checklists short. Use the guides below when you need a reminder.

## ☀️ Morning · Get ready

- [ ] **Teeth & tongue** — Brush with fluoride toothpaste for 2 minutes. Spit without rinsing immediately. Gently clean your tongue.
- [ ] **Shower & face** — Use warm water, Dove body wash, and CeraVe face cleanser. Follow the shower guide below. Shampoo only on wash days.
- [ ] **Dry & moisturize** — Pat dry. Dry skin folds and between toes carefully. Apply body lotion to slightly damp skin; avoid thick lotion in moist folds.
- [ ] **Underarms** — Apply deodorant or antiperspirant to completely dry skin, following the label.
- [ ] **SPF & lips** — Apply your SPF 30 facial moisturizer generously to face, ears, and neck. Use SPF 30 lip balm.
- [ ] **Hair & clothes** — Gently style your hair. Put on fresh underwear, socks, and a clean shirt once your feet are dry.

*Optional: a little fragrance, following the label. Avoid your face, groin, and irritated skin.*

## 🌙 Night · Wind down

- [ ] **Clean face** — Wash off sunscreen, oil, and dirt. Skip a repeat wash if you just washed your face in the shower.
- [ ] **Moisturize** — Apply CeraVe PM to face and neck; use a thin layer of Vaseline on lips.
- [ ] **Floss & brush** — Floss if you haven't already today. Brush for 2 minutes with fluoride toothpaste; spit without rinsing immediately.
- [ ] **Reset for tomorrow** — Wear clean sleepwear, set out fresh clothes, and hang your towel to dry.

*As needed: shower after sweating or getting dirty; gently clean your tongue. Use nighttime antiperspirant on dry underarms if directed by its label.*

## 🧺 Weekly · Fresh start

**My reset day:** ____________________

- [ ] Wash bedsheets and pillowcases.
- [ ] Wash and condition hair when due; gently detangle.
- [ ] Check, clean, and trim fingernails.
- [ ] Clean combs, brushes, and styling tools.
- [ ] Wash hats, durags, and bonnets as needed.
- [ ] Maintain facial hair with clean tools.
- [ ] Wipe shoes as needed; let them dry between wears.
- [ ] Check supplies and note what needs replacing.

**Between resets:** wash towels after about 3 uses, sooner if damp, dirty, or smelly. Replace your toothbrush about every 3–4 months, or sooner if worn.

## 🚿 Shower guide · Head to toe

1. **Hair — wash days only.** Shampoo the scalp, rinse, condition, and gently detangle. Start around once weekly for tightly coiled hair; adjust to your scalp and hair needs.
2. **Face.** Use clean hands and CeraVe cleanser. Gently massage for 30–60 seconds, rinse, and avoid scrubbing.
3. **Upper body.** Behind ears → neck → shoulders → chest → belly and belly button → back → arms → armpits.
4. **Lower body.** Legs → knees → ankles → feet → between toes.
5. **Private areas.** With clean hands, gently wash groin creases, the external penis, and scrotum. Use water on the head of the penis; mild, fragrance-free cleanser on outer skin only if tolerated. If uncircumcised, gently retract the foreskin, rinse underneath, and replace it.
6. **Buttocks & external anal area — last.** Use clean hands or a separate washcloth. Wash gently, rinse well, and keep soap outside the anus. Wash your hands afterward.

**Finish:** pat dry with a clean towel and use a separate clean face towel. Dry underarms, groin folds, buttock crease, and between toes thoroughly.

## Remember

- **Floss once daily.** Morning or night—pick the time you'll remember.
- **A second shower is only as needed.** You don't need two full showers every day.
- **Be gentle.** Avoid aggressive scrubbing, scraping, or pulling your hair.
- **Outdoors:** reapply sunscreen every 2 hours and after sweating or swimming, following its label.
- **Mouthwash is optional.** Use fluoride rinse at a separate time from brushing.

*The aim is consistency. Optional products can stay optional.*
